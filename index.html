Zero background bloat.

Modern clients run heavy background processes that consume massive amounts of your PC's memory before the game even starts. Swyft is built differently. It boots instantly, uses barely any RAM, and leaves all your hardware power exactly where it belongs: inside the game.

Our core principles.

We built Swyft because we were tired of the status quo. No bloatware, no locked ecosystems. Just raw engineering.

Independent.

We don't force you into partner networks or queue systems. Swyft is a neutral tool built to enhance your experience on any server, everywhere.

Maximum Frames.

Engineered for zero stutter and lowest input delay. We keep our footprint tiny so your PC can focus 100% of its power on running Minecraft smoothly.

Your data.
Your machine.

Trust is hard to earn and easy to lose, especially for a new project. We believe your PC is your domain, and gaming clients have no business profiling your hardware or tracking your behavior.

Swyft collects zero background telemetry. We run no hidden analytics and only communicate with our servers for essential authentication and version updates. What happens on your machine, stays on your machine.
