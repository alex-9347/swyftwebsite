3. Data Processing by the Client Software (Backend Services)

Our client and launcher follow the principle of data minimization ("Privacy by Design"). The client communicates with our server only to provide online status and multiplayer client features. We do not require an account with us, and we do not collect your name, email address or Minecraft credentials.

a) Online Status (Heartbeat Service)

  • Data processed: While the client is running, it sends an automated status update (HTTP POST) to our server approximately every 30 seconds. The request contains your Minecraft Player ID (UUID), a timestamp and your IP address, which is technically necessary to establish the connection. A Minecraft UUID can be linked to your player name through public services, so we treat it as personal data.
  • Purpose: Determining online activity to synchronize client status indicators and cosmetic features.
  • Storage and duration: UUIDs and timestamps are kept only in the server's volatile memory (RAM) and are not written to persistent storage (hard drives or databases). An automated cleanup routine removes inactive entries from RAM after a maximum of 120 seconds.
  • Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest is providing real-time client functionality that users expect from the client. We have weighed this interest against your rights and freedoms, taking into account that we process very few data, store them only for seconds and do not link them to other data. Because many Minecraft players are minors, we specifically considered their interests in this assessment.

b) In-Game Client Detection

  • Data processed: To show which players within your render distance also use the client, the software can send a list of the UUIDs of visible players (HTTP POST) to our server. This can include UUIDs of players who do not use our client.
  • Purpose: Checking which of the visible players currently have an active session held in RAM.
  • Storage and duration: The server performs a temporary lookup against active RAM sessions and returns the matching IDs. The submitted IDs are not logged or stored and are discarded immediately after the response is sent. We do not create profiles about who is near whom.
  • Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest is providing the client-detection feature. Because the processing is transient and no data is retained, the impact on the affected players is very low.
  • Information for affected third parties (Art. 14 GDPR): Players who do not use our client are not informed individually, because the data are discarded immediately and providing individual notice would be impossible or would require disproportionate effort (Art. 14(5)(b) GDPR). This section serves as public information.

c) Aggregate Online Counter

An HTTP GET endpoint returns the total number of currently active client users. It returns only a single number. No user IDs, UUIDs or other identifiers are transmitted or exposed.

d) Server Access Logs

We do not keep persistent HTTP access logs containing IP addresses for the backend endpoints described above. IP addresses are processed in volatile memory only for the duration of the connection.

e) Microsoft / Minecraft Login

The launcher lets you sign in with your Microsoft account. The sign-in takes place directly between your device and Microsoft; we do not receive your Microsoft password, and the login data are never transmitted to our servers.

After signing in, the launcher stores the resulting authentication tokens locally on your device so that you do not have to log in again each time. These tokens remain on your device, and we have no access to them. You can remove them at any time by deleting the launcher's data folder.

When you join a Minecraft server, the game client authenticates with Microsoft/Mojang services as usual. We are not involved in this process. Microsoft's privacy policy applies to the sign-in and to these services.

f) Mandatory Data and Automated Decisions

Using the online features requires the transmission of the data described above; without it, the online status and client detection features cannot work. We do not use automated decision-making or profiling within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR.
